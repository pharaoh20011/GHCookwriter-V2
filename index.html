04 — THE AUTHOR

George H. Cook

The Writer Behind The Encounter Trilogy

Born and raised in Southwest Kansas. A graduate of Protection High School in 1986, serving in the U.S. Navy from 1986–1995, traveling the world and experiencing the vibrancy and differences of many cultures around the globe as well as seeing many historical sites. He moved to Kansas City in 1999, marrying his wife Carmen in August 2001.

The Reform Party of Kansas nominated him for United States Senate – Kansas in both 2002 and 2004. Following those campaigns for public office, George and his wife Carmen focused on family, raising their four children.

This led to the writing of his first short story — I had a bad dream on a dark, stormy Night. As the children expressed their ideas for what they wanted to become, more short stories followed, culminating in his debut collection — Adventures in Dreamland — published in May 2022.

“What started as a love triangle concept in 1988 morphed into a compelling trilogy of fate, ambition, and redemption set in Kansas City.”

Thirty-one months of writing labor, even after overcoming a stroke, brought forth the first book of the trilogy — A Chance Encounter — published in November 2025 and now available for purchase. Book two — A Random Encounter — is also available for purchase. The third and final book in The Encounter Trilogy is currently in preview and available for pre-order.

Testimonials From Readers

What Readers & Reviewers Are Saying About The Encounter Trilogy

“

A wonderful contemporary romance for library shelves. A Chance Encounter is exactly the kind of story library patrons love: charming, emotional and easy to get lost in. Greg's imperfections make him compelling and Carry is a warm, bright presence from the moment she appears. The pacing is excellent and the corporate setting adds depth without overwhelming the romance. This book is going straight on my recommended reading list.

Eleanor B. Public Librarian
“

5 STAR REVIEW! A romance that feels alive. This book hooked me fast. The chemistry between Greg and Carry feels natural and Veronica's confidence adds the perfect amount of fire. Their moments at Club Euchre bring the whole world to life. Greg's flaws, especially his issues with drinking, make him relatable and human. And I cannot overlook the excellent side characters: Nurse Greta had me cracking up more than once.

Marcus J. Delaney Book Reviewer
“

A thoughtful mix of romance and life lessons. From an educator's point of view this book offers more than romance: it provides meaningful emotional lessons. Greg's journey from denial to accountability is portrayed with surprising nuance. The relationships, especially the trio of Greg, David, and Darren, highlight loyalty, conflict resolution, and personal growth. The Prom backstory and its lingering effects were handled excellently.
